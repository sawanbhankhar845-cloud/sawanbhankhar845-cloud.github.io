Privacy Policy for SA1 Register

Last updated: September 2026

SA1 Register ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed and maintained by Sawan Bhankhar. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

1. Data Storage

SA1 Register stores all your data — including Attendance Register, Stock Register, Cash Book, Day Book/Register, and Notes Register entries — locally on your device using an SQLite database. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal records, business data, or app content on any external server or cloud service.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, or any personal identification information through the app's core features.

3. Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including your device's Advertising ID, to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. This data collection is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy, which you can review here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You may opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings.

4. Permissions

The app may request permissions necessary for its core functionality, such as generating and saving PDF reports to your device. These permissions are used solely for that purpose and not for data collection.

5. Children's Privacy

SA1 Register is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security

Since your data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device's own security settings. We recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock and up-to-date software.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
